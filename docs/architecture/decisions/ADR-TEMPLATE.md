## Context

What problem or question forced this decision? What constraints, prior decisions, or
methodology requirements shaped it? Name the gap-register row this closes, if any. Include
what was actually investigated (a measured baseline, a tool tested for real, an API call
that confirmed or ruled something out) — not just assumptions. A decision grounded in real
investigation is far more trustworthy later than one that sounds plausible.

## Decision

State the decision plainly, in one or two sentences, before explaining it. Then explain the
reasoning — why this option over the alternatives that were considered.

## Consequences

**Benefits:**

- What this decision makes possible, or better, going forward.

**Trade-offs:**

- What this decision gives up, or makes harder — stated honestly, not minimised. A
  decision with no listed trade-offs usually means the trade-offs weren't looked for.
